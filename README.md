# 💫 About Me:
Hey there! 👋 I'm Srinjay Paul, a third-year undergraduate student (Class of 2027) studying Electronics and Tele-Communication Engineering at Jadavpur University in Kolkata. 

🌱 I am currently a summer research intern at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), focusing on Digital VLSI and Hardware for AI.
🔭 My core technical passions lie in VLSI design, FPGA-based systems, Computer Vision, and Digital Signal Processing. I also possess a deep, ongoing curiosity for the vast expanse of Astrophysics!
📸 When I'm not writing hardware description code or running MATLAB simulations, you can usually find me capturing portraits and landscapes through my camera lens, or enjoying a classic cup of gorom cha at a local cafe.
